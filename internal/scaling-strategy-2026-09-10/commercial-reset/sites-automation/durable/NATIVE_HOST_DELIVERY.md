It made zero model requests and zero production wallet writes. The implementation preserved unknown uploads and publication locks across restart. Tests covered duplicate handoffs, conflicting operation/source/archive/account fields, cancellation, mutable intent, duplicate acknowledgements and a single publication. The original record also includes separate supplier ceiling probes and the successful 16-token comparison receipt; neither established a hard supplier ceiling.

The native-host handoff remains the recorded successful delivery route. The direct CLI archive-save mechanism remains unqualified.
