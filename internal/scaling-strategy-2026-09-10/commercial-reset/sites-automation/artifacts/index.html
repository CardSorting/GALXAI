Northstar Studio Onboarding Portal

A fictional guide to starting, reviewing, and completing a design project with Northstar Studio. All details are illustrative and contain no real people, credentials, integrations, or customer data.

1. Kickoff

The kickoff aligns the studio and project team on goals, audience, scope, milestones, and decision-making roles. Share the brief, brand references, known constraints, and success measures. The studio confirms assumptions, records open questions, and publishes a simple plan for the first design sprint.

2. Weekly Review

Each weekly review covers progress, key decisions, risks, and next steps. The studio presents current concepts or prototypes, while the project team provides focused feedback against the agreed goals. Feedback should be specific, prioritized, and consolidated by the designated project coordinator before the next sprint begins.

3. Project Handover

At handover, the studio presents the approved final direction and summarizes remaining decisions, usage guidance, and known limitations. The team receives an organized project summary, final review notes, and a list of recommended follow-up actions. Both sides confirm acceptance, ownership, and the closing date for the engagement.
