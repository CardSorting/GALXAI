# Second-pass commercial audit

The central finding is a mismatch between the proposed value story and the evidence supporting its economics. Several weaknesses are in commercial drafts; others reach actual application surfaces. This distinction matters: an internal forecast is not a deployed promise, and a rendered promise is not proof of an activated entitlement.

Evidence is from revision `4587642`, source inspection, the earlier isolated production build and official market documentation accessed September 10, 2026. Severity indicates the consequence if the item is used to sell or scale the offer. No production customer records were inspected.

## Counterchecks

The present pricing FAQ is materially more careful than the legacy data: it separates plan fees from usage, requires support confirmation and avoids assumed universal caching. Existing payment fulfillment has durable idempotency and refund handling. The platform already has substantial admission, authentication and image-output protections. The right response is to complete and connect those assets, not declare the entire system unusable.

The investor documents have a `commercial-draft` scope notice. This reduces the risk of confusing them with a current runtime contract, but does not make their numbers appropriate inputs for an investment decision. Likewise, imported data can be eliminated or selectively projected during a build; a raw string in a source file is not proof of visible UI. This audit checked three prerendered pages with scripts and styles excluded.

The earlier asset scan covered eight selected internal markers. It did not cover these commercial assertions and never established that the live site was clean. The [new observations](render-observations.json) demonstrate that the build can pass those checks while still carrying unsupported commercial claims. A future copy change needs expanded assertions, affected-page inspection, browser-asset scanning, adversarial response tests, authorization tests and a production build.
