# Sites content (Retired)

<!-- docs-scope: current-guide -->

Sites is no longer offered for new website creation or subscriptions. The former
workspace links lead signed-in accounts to [GALX Images](https://galx.ai/images).
Foundry and Orbit do not include Sites.

Existing private work remains private. Historical access or an old receipt does
not establish current creation availability. Existing integrations retain their
separate access requirements; signing in alone does not grant integration access.

For current capabilities, visit [GALX](https://galx.ai).
