# Runbook template

<!-- docs-scope: domain-reference -->

Copy into the owning runbook directory. Replace every instruction below with a concrete procedure before registration; this template is not an operational recipe.

## Purpose and trigger

Name the user-visible symptom or desired operation, owner role and successful outcome.

## Authority and prerequisites

Name the exact component, environment, required authorization, tools, source and known-good state. Distinguish read-only diagnosis from mutations.

## Procedure

Give ordered, bounded steps with expected observations and branches. Link canonical configuration; do not duplicate secrets or volatile defaults.

## Stop conditions

State what evidence means stop, contain or escalate to the component owner. Preserve original operation identity on uncertain outcomes.

## Recovery and rollback

Describe compatible recovery, reversibility, retained state and when rollback is unsafe.

## Verification and evidence

Define acceptance, relevant checks and the private evidence record: date, target, revision, result, remaining risk and next review. Separate local fixtures from live acceptance.
