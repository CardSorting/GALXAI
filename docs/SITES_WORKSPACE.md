# Create and manage Sites

<!-- docs-scope: current-guide -->

Open [Sites](https://galx.ai/sites) to create a simple website through conversation. Your chat appears on the left and your page preview on the right. On a phone, use the Chat and Preview tabs to switch between them.

Activate GALX Pro in [Billing](https://galx.ai/billing#plan). After payment is confirmed, return to Sites. You can manage or cancel the subscription from Billing; cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period. API and image usage remain separate.

## Start with an idea

Sign in, describe the page you want, and send your request. You can start with a product launch, a portfolio or an event page. Creating, updating and publishing Sites is included with GALX Pro, subject to fair usage. There is no separate charge for each Site update. The workspace shows whether your account has access.

Review the draft, then describe what you want to change. Each turn builds on your current draft, project notes and a summary of earlier decisions. This release creates simple information pages; it does not create accounts, working checkout forms or custom application behavior for your visitors.

Your draft and conversation are saved to your account. Open My Sites to find, search or resume a Site. You can rename a Site or archive it. Archiving keeps an already published Site online; use Unpublish to take it offline.

## Keep improving through conversation

Use **Build** to apply a change to the current draft. Use **Discuss** to ask questions or explore an idea without changing the page. If a request needs clarification, answer in the same conversation and continue from there. Each reply explains what changed or answers your question.

Open **Context** to save project notes such as your audience, tone, important facts and things to preserve. These notes stay until you edit them. A conversation summary updates automatically as you chat. Summaries are selective: pin important details in your notes, and correct a mistaken summary through chat. Your notes and conversation stay private.

Your saved conversation stays with each Site. Use **Load earlier messages** to read older turns. New replies do not pull you away while you’re reading earlier messages; **Latest message** returns you to the end. The text you have not sent and your Build/Discuss choice stay with that Site in the same browser tab, including after a reload. These unsent drafts are local to that tab; saved messages are available from your account.

You can prepare your next message while a reply is pending. Send it once the current reply finishes. Switching Sites opens that Site’s own conversation and composer.

## Review and restore versions

A change to your page creates a saved version. Select a version in chat or open **History** to preview it. Discussions leave the page unchanged and do not create page versions.

Choose **Restore this version**, then confirm, to make an earlier page your current draft. Restoration saves a new version, so the draft you left remains in History. Your project notes stay in place, and future replies start from the restored draft. Your published page changes only when you publish again.

For Sites created before version history was introduced, History starts with the drafts that were still saved at the time. Older discarded conversations and versions cannot be recovered.

## Preview and publish

Use the desktop and phone controls to check the page at different widths. The initial example is illustrative; it is replaced by your own draft after your first completed update. Download exports the page you are previewing as a standalone HTML file. An older version includes its version number in the download name.

When publishing is available, choose Publish and review the sharing notice. Publishing makes the approved page available to anyone with the link. Your conversation remains private. Choose an available username the first time; your address will look like `my-product.alice.galx.ai`. You do not need a separate hosting account or a connection running on your computer for Sites created in this workspace.

Address setup continues automatically. Wait for the Site to become Live before sharing its link. Later edits are saved as drafts, and the published page changes only when you publish again. Unpublish removes the public page while keeping your draft and reserved address.

## Resume interrupted work

If an update is interrupted, reopen the Site to check its progress. Avoid submitting the same change again while it is still updating. If an update cannot produce a usable draft, your previous draft stays saved.

Generation and publishing may be unavailable for an account during rollout. The workspace shows their availability separately. Existing Sites created through another connected workflow retain their original access settings and setup requirements; they are not automatically imported into My Sites.

Fair usage helps keep Sites available across accounts. You may be asked to pause before another update. Access can become available again without waiting for a new billing period. Your saved work stays available while updates are paused.
