# Sites workspace (Retired)

<!-- docs-scope: current-guide -->

The Sites website builder is retired. Its workspace links now lead signed-in
accounts to [GALX Images](https://galx.ai/images). They no longer open the former
editor or a draft-export screen. New Sites subscriptions are unavailable;
Foundry and Orbit do not include Sites.

Existing records and previously assigned addresses are retained. Retirement
does not publish private drafts or transfer their ownership. An old subscription
or receipt does not establish current creation availability. Do not purchase
another plan expecting it to restore the Sites workspace.

For current capabilities and account options, visit [GALX](https://galx.ai).
