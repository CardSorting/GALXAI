Yeah. That concern is actually legitimate, but I think the reason you can't explain why is that there are two different things getting conflated.

If a large portion of your potential audience has:

“AI = Claude Code”

as their dominant mental model, that's potentially a category-level HCI problem, not merely a messaging problem.

Because a mental model determines what people even recognize as a possible solution.

If they believe the world is:

User → Claude Code → task completed

then anything outside that loop can become invisible to them.

They aren't necessarily rejecting GALXAI. They may be unable to place it in their ontology of AI products.

And that's much harder than explaining a feature.

But here's where I think your uncertainty is important:

You don't yet know that this is a user problem.

You know that one investor appeared to have an extremely Claude-Code-centric reference frame.

That's evidence of the phenomenon.

It isn't evidence that your target developers share it.

Your 20-person conversations are actually relevant here. If those people could understand “we reduce AI API costs” without needing Claude Code as an anchor, then you've already observed people operating with a broader mental model.

So the hypothesis isn't:

“Claude Code users can't understand GALXAI.”

It's:

“Does a Claude-Code-centric mental model prevent otherwise relevant users from recognizing the problem GALXAI solves?”

That's testable.

And there's a really clean HCI experiment hiding here:

Give someone only the outcome:

“GALXAI reduces AI computation cost without reducing output quality or speed.”

Don't mention Claude Code.

Then ask:

“What do you think this product does?”

If they produce something wildly different from what GALXAI actually does, then you have a mental-model problem.

If they immediately understand the proposition, the investor's Claude Code fixation may be irrelevant to your audience.

And that distinction matters enormously.

Because otherwise you risk doing exactly what you were worried about earlier: redesigning GALXAI around the mental model of one person who may simply have had a weirdly narrow frame of reference. 😂

So I think your instinct is right:

“If users literally only understand AI through Claude Code, that's a much bigger problem.”

But the thing you don't know yet is whether that describes your users.

And that is probably the piece your HCI brain is trying to nail down.
