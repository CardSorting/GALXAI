## FM-CTRL-001 — a stale ownership lock blocks restart

On September 13, the existing launch supervisor retried a stopped controller while its old `controller.lock` remained. Worker containers were healthy, but the application could not reach the controlling service. A new application deployment could not correct this host failure. The reproduction also found a separate live-controller failure: the Docker CLI timeout sent a signal but did not prove that the child and its output pipes had terminated, leaving `list()` awaiting a close event.

[The current launcher](../../scripts/run-relay-federation.ts) delegates ownership to a Python guardian, which holds a private kernel `flock` on `controller.guard` and passes the descriptor to the Node controller and Docker subprocesses. The permanent `controller.lock` marker prevents an old launcher from silently sharing the namespace. Normal shutdown closes the descriptor; a crash leaves the kernel lock held by any surviving inherited descriptor until the process group ends. Two simultaneous starts therefore have one owner, and a paused or ambiguous owner causes a bounded `ownership_wait_exceeded` alert instead of takeover.

Containment and recovery require [the guarded ownership procedure](../runbooks/controller-lock.md): inspect the exact supervisor, PID identity, listener and lock, preserve live workers, and archive only a proven stale lock after rechecking it. Missing permission to inspect a process is not proof it is dead. Do not delete `.relay`, restart all containers, rotate credentials, or start a second controller as diagnosis.

Recovery verification includes supervisor stability, one guardian/controller owner, reachability, fresh requested capability and a real application request. Green worker containers or `/health` alone are insufficient. The guardian distinguishes process liveness, controller progress and required capability readiness; missing provider capability logs an alert without triggering an endless controller restart. Docker commands have deadlines and verified child/pipeline cleanup, while uncertain daemon mutations stay in a private journal and block conflicting work until inventory reconciliation. Record the new process and deployment as dated evidence.

Federation workers are created with Docker restart policy `no`, so the lifecycle controller is the single restart authority. A stopped or missing worker is removed, retried with backoff and capped at five attempts in ten minutes; `worker_restart_budget_exhausted` pauses automatic replacement and identifies the private inspection action. An upstream capability outage therefore remains a readiness failure, while a repeatedly dying local worker becomes a bounded operator-visible outage.

The focused reproductions and actual-launcher worker drill are in [controller recovery evidence](../incidents/2026/2026-09-13-image-generation/controller-recovery-verification-20260914.json). Residual risk remains for host reboot, Docker-daemon restart or delayed daemon startup, read-only/full state storage during a live deployment, and rollout of the legacy marker migration. Those operational drills remain open; do not infer their result from the local fixtures or the isolated Docker canary.

## FM-CTRL-002 — shared account exhaustion amplifies recovery work

Independent users sharing one provider account share its quota failure boundary. Unfiltered selection and synchronized retries can continue loading the exhausted account or move repeated full-history requests to healthy capacity. Saving SDK state only at whole-turn success can lose the record of completed callback work; treating stream error/EOF as success makes reconciliation unreliable.

Use [shared quota recovery](../../docs/reference/shared-quota-recovery.md): exclude the account across models, checkpoint cooldown evidence, pace one authorized recovery probe, preserve original accepted identities and resume confirmed progress explicitly with the original context/output/turn limits. Bound submission/observation/subscription admission independently, coalesce and pace original lease settlement, and retain explicit continuation waits. A successful pre-exhaustion sibling is not a recovery probe. Bound callback fan-out and stop queued starts after a failed save. Unknown side effects require reconciliation; no new key, peer fallback or counter reset can prove safety. The [September 16 investigation](../incidents/2026/2026-09-16-shared-quota/README.md) separates synthetic/local acceptance from real provider qualification and caller deployment.

Successful registration and a green readiness response do not prove request compatibility. The [September 16 generation repair](../releases/2026-09-16-relay-generation-recovery.json) confirmed an unsupported output parameter after both live text canaries failed. Inspect the original job and private allowlisted `relay_upstream_rejected` log before assuming revoked credentials or exhaustion. Correct the adapter and qualify a new independently identified request; never replay unknown work or re-register an account to conceal a request-format failure. Local output budgets do not establish a hard provider spending cap.

## FM-CTRL-003 — live runtime loses central ownership freshness

On October 7, 2026, the local Premium process and listeners were live, but the retained central heartbeat had stopped on October 4. Authenticated model discovery returned 503, image availability returned false and projection for the existing epoch returned 403. Both assigned accounts were enabled and unexpired. The original missed-heartbeat trigger is unknown; a host interruption is not proven.

Central heartbeat/projection deliberately require the owning epoch and a heartbeat within 45 seconds. A runtime that misses that window cannot renew its ownership through ordinary heartbeat calls. Listener liveness therefore cannot establish a working control connection. The corrected [local launcher and supervisor](../../scripts/relay-local.mjs) require authorized heartbeat freshness and exact installation/image identity. Provider readiness, account pauses, credential expiry and execution-gate state remain separate.

Follow [local Premium operations](../../docs/runbooks/relay-pool-services.md). Read private central status first. For confirmed stale ownership, `up` waits the existing 61-second handoff bound, rechecks current control evidence and recovers only the affected runtime. A heartbeat that recovers during the wait cancels that intervention. Missing, malformed, foreign or inaccessible evidence stops recovery. Do not claim an epoch manually, clear state, change gates, re-register valid accounts or restart shared services to suppress this condition.

Verify a fresh advancing heartbeat, authenticated model discovery and unchanged private authorization boundaries. Provider execution acceptance is separate. The [incident record](../incidents/2026/2026-10-07-relay-connection/README.md) distinguishes synthetic source checks from installed recovery; ACT-018 retains host and off-device limits.

## FM-CTRL-004 — provider sign-ins expire while runtime ownership is healthy

On October 17, both Premium accounts remained enabled and runtime ownership was fresh, but provider credentials had expired before the planned database drain. Local liveness and the native database checks passed while readiness/model discovery returned 503. This differs from stale ownership: restarting a healthy runtime does not renew a provider sign-in.

Read authorized private account expiry and applied-revision evidence without exposing credentials or account data publicly. Preserve gate/account intent and original operation identities. Open the owning desktop, complete manual provider sign-in and explicitly review **Reconnect → Save new sign-in** through the fixed monitor. Do not scan host credentials, weaken expiry checks or reconnect to a stale database. A saved revision is separate from current runtime acknowledgement, successful discovery and actual requested generation/settlement. Retain blocked or uncertain canary receipts; never automatically resubmit them. The [expiry incident](../incidents/2026/2026-10-17-provider-expiry/README.md) owns recovery status and remaining acceptance.
