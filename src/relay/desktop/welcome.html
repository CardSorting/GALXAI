A browser of your own.

Use the address bar to sign in and configure your services. This Chrome profile, its downloads and your terminal files stay in this workspace.

Open Terminal from the Dock to install tools and configure your relay. Run galx-relay for guided setup, start, status and stop commands. The local relay runs inside this desktop; it does not change the serving fleet. Files in ~/workspace persist when you stop and resume this desktop.

The desktop runs Linux with familiar window controls. It is separate from the host computer and other relay desktops.
